# Security Notes

- Passwords are entered through `getpass` so terminal echo is disabled.
- Passwords are not included in reports or persisted by this application.
- Do not submit real passwords in screenshots, issues, logs, or test fixtures.
- The built-in list and detection rules are intentionally limited and may miss weak passwords.
- The score is educational and heuristic; do not use it as the sole authentication control.
- This version performs no network requests and no breach-database lookups.
